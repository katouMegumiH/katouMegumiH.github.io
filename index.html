ABOUT ME

Understanding the brain.
Decoding its representations.

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology Beijing. My research sits at the intersection of NeuroAI, multimodal neuroimaging, and generative modeling.

I work on reconstructing visual experience from fMRI and EEG, and on modeling brain connectivity in autism and neurodegenerative diseases. I am particularly interested in how complementary neural signals and pretrained models can be combined to build more transferable brain decoders.

My broader work explores multimodal representation learning, model merging, and reasoning under linguistic uncertainty.

Research interests

01

Multimodal brain decoding

Learning complementary fMRI and EEG representations to condition video diffusion models. I explore modality-specific adaptation and weight-space merging to bring spatial and temporal neural information into a shared decoding pipeline.

fMRI / EEG · Diffusion models · LoRA
02

Brain connectivity & disease modeling

Studying functional connectivity, neural dynamics, and structure–function relationships in ASD, Alzheimer’s disease, and Parkinson’s disease, with an emphasis on spatiotemporal learning and structure-guided denoising.

Connectomics · Transformers · Neuroimaging
03

Model merging & multimodal representations

Combining complementary biological vision–language models through training-free, layer-wise search. I also study fuzzy semantic modeling for language-model reasoning under uncertainty, as part of a broader interest in reliable and transferable representations.

Foundation models · Model merging · Uncertainty
